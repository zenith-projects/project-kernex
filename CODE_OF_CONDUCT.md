# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free experience for everyone contributing to PROJECT KERNEX, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

### Positive behavior includes:

- Being respectful and constructive in code reviews and discussions
- Giving and gracefully accepting feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the project and community
- Showing empathy and kindness toward other contributors
- Crediting others' work and ideas appropriately

### Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment, trolling, or personal attacks
- Discriminatory language or jokes
- Publishing others' private information without consent
- Spamming issues, PRs, or discussions
- Any behavior that would be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainer at **zenithprojects.dev@gmail.com**.

All reports will be reviewed and investigated. The maintainer reserves the right to remove, edit, or reject contributions and to ban temporarily or permanently any contributor whose behavior violates this Code of Conduct.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including the GitHub repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, Discord server, and any other official communication channel.
